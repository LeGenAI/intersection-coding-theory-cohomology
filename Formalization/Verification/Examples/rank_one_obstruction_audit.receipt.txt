interpretation:
  Every Euclidean self-dual code over GF(5) of lengths 8 and 10 is
  permutation equivalent to a representative with r=1. Indeed, every code
  has an information set and hence a systematic generator (I_N | A) with
  A A^T = -I_N; the audit enumerates every such ordered orthogonal A and every
  oriented pairing until it finds an r=1 witness. Consequently neither
  length can supply a permutation-invariant rank-two example.
